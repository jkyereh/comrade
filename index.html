About Us

We set out to offer help to the needy and ease the burden on low-income households. Little did we know that our core mandate would increase to encapsulate more than just helping. Over the years, we've engaged in community projects and established relationships that makes our work more engaging and socially rewarding. We are a Non-Profit Organization with humanity at heart, and a focus on changing lives through every way possible.

Support Us Projects

Uniform and clothing

We mobilize support of school aged children with regards to school uniforms. These are usually used uniforms but go a long way to put smiles on the faces of these kids .

Food and livelihood

It becomes necessary sometimes to support our communities with food and a means to livelihood. The hardships in some of these communities require external assistance to meet the nutritional needs of community members.

Skills Empowerment

We don't only give our beneficiaries fishes, we teach them to fish too. Our wide range of skills training makes it possible for the needy and other community members to learn a valuable skill, trade or profession. These can range from simple Soap-making skills to apprenticeship in the fashion design profession.
