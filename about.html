About Us

We set out to offer help to the needy and ease the burden on low-income households. Little did we know that our core mandate would increase to encapsulate more than just helping. Over the years, we've engaged in community projects and established relationships that makes our work more engaging and socially rewarding. We are a Non-Profit Organization with humanity at heart, and a focus on changing lives through every way possible.

Everyone deserves a chance in life, the poor and needy deserve even more. We can't promise to solve every problem out there, but we can do our bit to solve the onnes within our reach. The least we can do is to provide basic amenities such as clothing, food and water. And we'll do so relentlessly and with joy. We have a duty to humanity, to give our fellows the same level of comfort we enjoy.

Brief History

How it all started

Comrades Association was started by Mr. Asante our Director, 8 yrs ago from a lifelong passion of helping people due to the help he also received as a child. He came from a family of 8 boys where clothing was a scarce commodity. The brothers had to hand donw their clothing to the younger ones once they outgrew the clothes. Growing up and realizing others were faced with much similar situations, he sought to help them however possible. When he had the chance to travel to the UK, his dreams of helping the needy finally became a reality as he was able to amass support for this task. He sees to the shipping of the clothes to Ghana himself, and follows up by going to arrange transport on the ground in Ghana to tranport the clothes and items to remote areas and needy communities. His travels to deliver these clothes even opend him up more to the other harsh realities of life and how needy others were. It was at this point that he decided to fully make it his dream to mobilize and support the needy.
